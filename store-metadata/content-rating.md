## Google Play Data Safety

### Data collected directly by the app

- Local gameplay data is stored on the device to run progress, missions, achievements, and settings.
- Optional Supabase cloud features send an anonymised player identifier, display name, score, category, and timestamp. Magic-link sign-in adds an email only when the player chooses to sign in.
- Optional AdMob rewarded ads may process advertising identifiers, diagnostics, approximate location, and ad interaction data under Google's policies.

### Data sharing

- Supabase: optional leaderboard, sync, and friend challenge data.
- Google AdMob: only when ads are enabled and shown.
- Apple App Store or Google Play: purchase processing for Remove Ads.

### User controls

- Players can avoid cloud features by playing offline and not signing in or syncing.
- Players can limit ad tracking in device settings and remove ads through the store purchase when available.
- Players can clear local data through device app settings.
