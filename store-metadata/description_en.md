## Short Description (≤ 80 chars — Play Store)
Test your Tanzania knowledge! 600+ questions across 10 categories.

## Long Description

**Mtaa Quiz Battle** is a Swahili-first trivia game celebrating Tanzanian culture, history, music, food, wildlife, and street life. Challenge yourself with 600+ hand-crafted bilingual questions across 10 themed categories.

### Key Features

🏆 **Daily Challenge** — A new question set every day to keep your streak alive and your knowledge sharp.

🎯 **10 Categories** — Football, Bongo Fleva music, Tanzanian history, regions, food, Swahili language, Dar es Salaam streets, wildlife, business, and general knowledge.

📈 **Player Progression** — Climb 9 ranks (Mgeni → Hadithi), unlock 17 achievements, and track your mastery bar for every category.

🌍 **Cloud Leaderboard** — Compare scores with players worldwide on the global leaderboard, or keep it local.

⚡ **Multiple Modes** — Standard quiz, Daily Challenge, timed Sprint, and Versus mode.

🔔 **Daily Reminders** — Optional notifications to keep your daily streak going.

🌐 **Bilingual** — Every question available in both Swahili and English — great for learners and locals alike.

🌙 **Dark / Light Theme** — Switch between themes to suit your preference.

📡 **Works Offline** — No internet required to play. All questions stored on-device.
