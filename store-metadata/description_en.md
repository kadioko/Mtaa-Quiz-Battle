# Mtaa Quiz Battle Store Description (English)

## App Name

Mtaa Quiz Battle

## Short Description (80 characters or fewer)

Test Tanzania knowledge with 626 bilingual questions, daily missions, and more.

## Long Description

Mtaa Quiz Battle is a Swahili-first trivia game made for Tanzania. Test yourself across music, football, history, regions, food, language, wildlife, business, and everyday street knowledge with 626 hand-crafted questions in Swahili and English.

### Play Your Way

- Classic quizzes, Daily Challenge, Weekly Challenge, Sprint, Versus, Friend Challenge, and Practice Mistakes.
- Daily missions with coin rewards for playing rounds, correct answers, and answer streaks.
- Smart Home recommendations that point you to a weak category, unresolved mistakes, or a fresh category to explore.
- Review every answer after a round, read the explanation, and practise missed questions.

### Grow Your Profile

- Earn coins, collect daily rewards, and climb 10 player ranks.
- Unlock 27 achievements and see newly earned badges immediately after a round.
- Track category mastery, accuracy, streaks, best scores, and recent history.
- Choose light or dark mode and play in Swahili or English.

### Tanzania at the Centre

Explore 10 categories: Simba na Yanga, Bongo Fleva, Tanzania History, Regions of Tanzania, Tanzanian Foods, Swahili Language, Streets of Dar, Wildlife and Parks, Business and Hustle, and General Knowledge TZ.

### Optional Connected Features

Play fully offline. When cloud features are enabled, you can compare scores on global and regional leaderboards, pick your mkoa for the Mikoa league, sync progress, sign in by magic link, and challenge friends with a share code.

Mtaa Quiz Battle is built to help you play, learn, and represent your mtaa.
