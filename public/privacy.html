Mtaa Quiz Battle Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 10, 2026

Mtaa Quiz Battle is a trivia game about Tanzania. This policy explains what data the app uses and how players can contact us.

Data Stored On Device

The app stores gameplay data locally on the player's device, including username, avatar, scores, quiz history, streaks, coins, achievements, category progress, app settings, and Remove Ads purchase status.

Advertising

If ads are enabled and the player has not purchased Remove Ads, the app may use Google AdMob to show rewarded ads. Google may process device identifiers, advertising identifiers, approximate location, diagnostics, and ad interaction data according to Google's policies.

In-App Purchases

The app offers a Remove Ads purchase. Purchase processing is handled by Google Play or Apple App Store. Mtaa Quiz Battle does not receive full payment card details.

Notifications

If the player enables reminders, the app may schedule daily challenge notifications. Players can disable notifications in app settings or device settings.

Cloud Features

If cloud leaderboard or sync features are enabled, the app may send the minimum required gameplay data, such as display name, score, category, and timestamp, to support those features. Do not enter sensitive personal information as a username.

Children

The app is intended as an educational trivia game suitable for general audiences. It does not knowingly collect sensitive personal information from children.

Data Deletion

Players can reset local app data from Settings. Store purchase records are managed by Google Play or Apple App Store and can be restored through the app when supported by the platform.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the app publisher through the support email listed on the Play Store or App Store listing.
